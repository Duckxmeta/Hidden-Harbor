# Photos Needed for Hidden Harbor Marina Website

To maintain authentic branding and eliminate all stock photography, the following real photos from Hidden Harbor Marina are requested for future upload:

1. **Pontoons (`pontoons`)**
   - High-resolution photo of a Hidden Harbor pontoon boat (especially double-deckers with water slides) on Center Hill Lake or at the dock.
2. **Houseboats (`houseboats`)**
   - Photo of a Hidden Harbor houseboat anchored in Casey Cove or on Center Hill Lake.
3. **Cabins (`cabins`)**
   - Exterior and interior shots of the lakeside cabin rentals at Casey Cove.
4. **Campsites (`campsite`)**
   - Photo of the 22 water/electric RV campsites or primitive tent spots nestled in the woods.
5. **Boat Slips & Dock (`slips`)**
   - Close-up or walkway photo of the covered/uncovered boat slips, fuel dock, and ship's store.

*Note: In the meantime, these sections utilize custom lake & cedar color blocks with descriptive typography as placeholders.*
